Independent witness record

Every five minutes an independent witness checks that diagnos' transparency logs only ever grow, and cosigns what it saw. Every hour an outside time-stamping authority dates the whole record. Hourly independent proof

Each hour the witness hashes the latest tree head of every log together with the previous hour's record, and asks an RFC 3161 time-stamping authority to sign that hash with the time. Deleting or rewriting any past hour breaks every link after it.

Witness history

One row per cosigned checkpoint: the witness only writes a record when a tree grew, with the proof that the new tree extends the old one.

How to verify

Don't take this page's word for it. These commands check the selected record with your own tools; VERIFY.md explains each step and what it proves.

What this record proves

It proves

  • Each log only grew: every cosigned checkpoint extends the previous one, by a Merkle consistency proof anyone can recheck.
  • The log showed the witness one history: two different trees for the same size would be signed evidence, published as a finding.
  • When: an outside authority dated the whole record every hour, so it cannot be rewritten later without breaking the chain.

It does not prove

  • What is inside the logs. Leaves are salted commitments; this repository never holds an entry or any personal data.
  • That diagnos logged everything it should. A witness sees the log, not the systems that write to it.
  • That the witness is honest on its own. That is why every record carries the log's own signature and a proof you can recheck against the log.
